## Production Notes

**Style guide**:
- Narration voice: warm, friendly, approachable — same tone as Kivi
- Pace: slightly slower than adult content — allow children time to look at visuals
- Repetition: key concepts stated twice (once in explanation, once in summary)
- Avoid: condescension, pressure language ("Quick! Time's running out!"), adult humour

**Animation/visual style**:
- Consistent with Kivora world-specific colour palette
- Characters: rounded, friendly, child-safe
- No realistic violence, scary elements, or adult themes

**Caption requirements**:
- Auto-generated first, then human-reviewed word by word
- Colour-coded by speaker if multiple speakers
- Font size minimum 32px equivalent at 1080p
- Background contrast ratio ≥ 4.5:1
