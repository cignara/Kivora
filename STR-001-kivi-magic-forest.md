# Kivi and the Magic Forest

---

## Cover & Title Screen

**Cover illustration**:
Kivi (KV-007 style — curious, adventurous posture) stands at the edge of a glowing forest. The trees are rounded, friendly shapes in warm greens and teals with tiny glowing fireflies. The forest path is visible but mysterious. Kivi's tail is raised with curiosity. Title text in Baloo 2 ExtraBold across the bottom. Warm, golden evening light. Inviting, not scary.

**Narrator intro** (KV-007, audio):
> "Kivi and the Magic Forest. A story about being curious and brave!"

---

## Story Pages

---

### Page 1

**Illustration description**:
Kivi stands in a sunny clearing at the edge of the forest. Bright flowers dot the grass. The forest entrance glows with warm light — visible but not entered yet. Kivi has a small backpack on. Expression: wide-eyed, curious.

**Text** (on-screen):
> One sunny day, Kivi found a magic forest.

**Audio narration**:
> "One sunny day, Kivi was out exploring when something amazing appeared — a magic forest! The trees glowed with a warm, gentle light. Kivi had never seen anything like it."

**Interactive elements**:
Tap the glowing forest to make the fireflies swirl briefly.

---

### Page 2

**Illustration description**:
Close-up of Kivi's face looking at the forest, eyes wide and bright. A small question mark floats in a thought bubble above Kivi's head.

**Text** (on-screen):
> "What's in there?" Kivi wondered.

**Audio narration**:
> "What could be inside? Were there new friends? Hidden treasures? Kivi's tail wagged with curiosity."

**Interactive elements**:
None — pure story read.

---

### Page 3

**Illustration description**:
Kivi takes one step into the forest. The trees are tall but friendly — rounded crowns, no sharp shapes. Bioluminescent mushrooms line the path. The path is clearly visible ahead, inviting.

**Text** (on-screen):
> Kivi felt a little scared. But Kivi stepped in anyway.

**Audio narration**:
> "Kivi's heart beat a little faster. The forest was big and new. But Kivi took a deep breath… and stepped in anyway. That's what being brave means — doing something even when you feel a little scared."

**Interactive elements**:
Tap Kivi to hear Kivi's heartbeat sound (gentle, not alarming). Tap again to continue.

---

### Page 4

**Illustration description**:
Inside the forest. Golden light filters through the canopy. A small glowing creature — a round, friendly beetle with a lantern on its head — peeks from behind a tree root. Kivi looks at it with delight, not fear.

**Text** (on-screen):
> A little bug with a lantern said, "Hello!"

**Audio narration**:
> "Then — something moved! A tiny creature with a glowing lantern peeped out from behind a tree. 'Hello!' it said, in a voice as small as a whisper."

**Interactive elements**:
Tap the beetle to make it wave its tiny legs and make a friendly chirp sound.

---

### ⭐ COMPREHENSION MOMENT 1 — placed at ~50% (between pages 4 and 5)

**Type**: prediction
**Prompt** (audio + simple on-screen icon):
> "What do you think Kivi will do? Touch your answer!"
**Visual**: Two big illustrated buttons:
  - Left: Kivi smiling and waving at the beetle
  - Right: Kivi running away
**Story continues regardless of choice.**
**Kivi response if left (wave)**: "Kivi waved hello — and so would you!" *(story continues)*
**Kivi response if right (run)**: "That could happen! But let's see what Kivi really did…" *(story continues to page 5)*

---

### Page 5

**Illustration description**:
Kivi and the little beetle are now sitting together on a glowing log. The beetle points along the forest path, where more creatures are visible in the distance — a friendly owl, a small deer, other fireflies.

**Text** (on-screen):
> The bug said, "I'll show you my forest friends!"

**Audio narration**:
> "'My name is Lumi!' said the little beetle. 'And I would love to show you my friends! They're just ahead.' Kivi smiled. Sometimes the best adventures start with a simple hello."

**Interactive elements**:
Tap the distant creatures to make them wave or glow briefly.

---

### Page 6

**Illustration description**:
Kivi and Lumi walk along the glowing path together. The forest feels warmer and friendlier now. More creatures peek from the trees, watching curiously. Kivi's expression has shifted from nervous to happy.

**Text** (on-screen):
> Together they walked deeper into the forest.

**Audio narration**:
> "And so Kivi and Lumi walked together, deeper into the magic forest. With a friend beside you, even new places feel safe."

**Interactive elements**:
Tap the path ahead to reveal the next page.

---

### ⭐ COMPREHENSION MOMENT 2 — placed at ~75%

**Type**: observation
**Prompt** (audio):
> "How does Kivi feel NOW? Touch the face that matches!"
**Visual**: Three large emoji-style faces:
  - 😊 Happy
  - 😨 Scared
  - 😴 Sleepy
**Correct answer**: Happy (though all responses are accepted with Kivi commentary)
**Kivi on happy**: "Happy! Kivi is NOT scared anymore. A new friend changes everything!"
**Kivi on scared**: "Hmm — but look at Kivi's face! Does Kivi look scared now? Look again…"
**Kivi on sleepy**: "Ha! Not sleepy — Kivi is way too excited for that!"

---

### Page 7

**Illustration description**:
A clearing filled with friendly forest creatures: the owl on a branch, the small deer eating berries, fireflies in spirals, Lumi front and centre. They're all looking at Kivi with welcoming, curious faces. A warm campfire glow in the centre. This is the emotional peak of the story — belonging and welcome.

**Text** (on-screen):
> All the forest friends said, "Welcome, Kivi!"

**Audio narration**:
> "In the centre of the magic forest was a beautiful clearing, full of the most wonderful creatures. They all looked at Kivi and said, together — WELCOME, KIVI! Kivi's heart was so full, it could have floated right up to the treetops."

**Interactive elements**:
Tap any creature to hear it say "welcome" in a different voice (owl hoots it, deer bleats it, fireflies chime it).

---

### Page 8 — Final page

**Illustration description**:
Kivi sitting among the new friends in the clearing as the forest glows around them. Lumi is on Kivi's shoulder. Stars appear in the sky through the canopy — it's early evening now. Kivi looks peaceful and happy. This is a warm, complete ending.

**Text** (on-screen):
> Kivi was so glad to be curious and brave.

**Audio narration**:
> "That evening, Kivi stayed and played with all the new friends. And Kivi thought: if I hadn't been a little bit brave… I would have missed all of this. Being curious, and taking that first step — that's how every adventure begins."

**Interactive elements**:
Tap the stars as they appear — each tap reveals one star with a soft chime.

---

## Story End & Kivi Reflection

**Final illustration**:
Same scene as Page 8, but slightly zoomed out to show the whole clearing. Warm and complete.

**Narrator wrap-up**:
> "Kivi discovered that curiosity is a superpower — and a new friend made the forest feel like home."

> **Kivi reflection prompt** (KV-007, speech bubble):
> "Have YOU ever tried something new and found a friend?"

**Child response options** (all equally valid — no wrong answer):
1. 🙋 "Yes! I made a new friend!"
2. 😊 "Not yet — but I want to!"
3. 🤔 "I'm still thinking about it!"
