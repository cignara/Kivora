Vision
Kivora is a child-safe, gamified, adaptive learning ecosystem for children aged 2–11 (Toddler to Grade 5), combining learning, creativity, exploration, storytelling, games, videos, coloring, printables, and life skills into a single adventure-based platform.

?? PATCH v2.1 — Kivi the Fox — Mascot Specification (New Section)
Mascot: Kivi the Fox
Kivi is Kivora's official mascot, guide, and emotional anchor for every child on the platform. Kivi is a friendly, curious, energetic fox — always present, always encouraging, never scary, never pressuring. Kivi's role is to make every child feel that they have a companion alongside them as they learn.

Kivi Character Pillars
• Curious — Kivi is always discovering something new and invites the child to discover it with them.
• Encouraging — Kivi celebrates every win, big or small. Kivi never judges, never shames.
• Funny — Kivi has a gentle sense of humour. Puns, silly dances, surprised expressions. Age-appropriate only.
• Safe — Kivi is predictable. Children know what to expect from Kivi, which builds trust.

Kivi Speech Rules
• Maximum 12 words per speech bubble — children do not read long instructions
• Grade 1–2 reading level maximum for all Kivi copy (Flesch-Kincaid check required)
• Always use exclamation marks for positive moments: "You're amazing!" not "Good work."
• Always use "we" framing for difficulty: "Let's try together!" not "Try again."
• No pressure language: never 'Quick!', 'Hurry!', or 'Time's running out!'
• Personalise when child name is available: "You're amazing, Asel!"

Rewards Economy
The rewards system has three layers: XP (progress tracking), Gems (premium earned currency), and Coins (daily-use earned currency). These are not interchangeable and must never be described as real-money equivalents in any child-facing copy.

Reward Controls
• Parents can set a daily XP cap (override platform defaults)
• Parents can disable Gem earning without affecting XP/badges
• Parents can disable Coin economy entirely
• Parents can require approval before rewards are redeemed
• Teachers can create custom classroom badges and award manually
• School admins can toggle the rewards system institution-wide
• No real-money purchases in the child portal — all rewards are earned, never bought
