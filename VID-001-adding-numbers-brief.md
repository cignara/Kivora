## Part 2 — Hook Scene (0:08–1:08)

> **Purpose**: Create the question BEFORE teaching. Surprising scenario first — then we explain.

**Concept**: Kivi is setting up a picnic on Math Mountain and has two baskets of fruit. Kivi keeps mixing them together and doesn't know how many fruits there are in total. A friendly number question hangs in the air — answered only in Part 3.

**Script**:

```
[Scene opens on Math Mountain picnic blanket. Kivi is unpacking two small baskets.]

NARRATOR: Kivi was setting up a picnic on Math Mountain today.
But there was a problem.

[Kivi looks at first basket — 3 apples. Then looks at second basket — 4 apples.]

KIVI: "I have apples here... and apples there!
But if I put them ALL together... how many do I have?!"

[Kivi dumps both baskets together. Apples roll everywhere. Kivi looks confused but amused.]

NARRATOR: When you put numbers together... that's called ADDING.
And by the end of this video, YOU will know exactly how many apples Kivi has.
Are you ready?
```

**Visual description**:
```
Shot 1 [0:08–0:18]: Wide shot of Math Mountain picnic. Warm afternoon light. Kivi arrives carrying two baskets.
Shot 2 [0:18–0:30]: Close-up: first basket with 3 bright apples. Kivi counts them with a paw — "one, two, three."
Shot 3 [0:30–0:42]: Close-up: second basket with 4 apples. Kivi counts — "one, two, three, four."
Shot 4 [0:42–1:00]: Medium shot: Kivi upturns both baskets together. Apples bounce in slow motion. Question mark appears above Kivi's head. Narrator asks the hook question.
Shot 5 [1:00–1:08]: Freeze frame on the pile of apples. Text on screen: "?" — holds 3 seconds. Then transitions to Core.
```

**On-screen text**: Only the "?" at the freeze frame — 2 words max, spoken simultaneously.
**Background music**: Curious, light — adventure theme. Low volume, fades under dialogue.

## Part 3 — Core Teaching (1:08–4:48)

### Segment 3.1 — What Does "Adding" Mean? (1:08–2:15)

**Duration**: ~67 seconds
**Concept**: Adding means joining two groups together to make one bigger group.

**Script**:
```
NARRATOR: Adding means putting two groups together.
Let's start with something simple.

[Scene: 2 stars on the left side of screen. 3 stars on the right side.]

NARRATOR: Kivi has 2 stars here...
and 3 stars over there.

[Stars slide together in the centre with a satisfying "click" sound.]

NARRATOR: When Kivi puts them ALL together...

[Stars are now one group of 5. A large numeral "5" appears.]

NARRATOR: ...Kivi has 5 stars!
Two stars... plus three stars... equals five!

[On screen: "2 + 3 = 5" appears in large Baloo 2 font, each number appearing as it is spoken.]

NARRATOR: Plus means we're adding.
Equals means this is the answer.
```

**Visual description**:
```
Shot 1 [1:08–1:25]: Two groups of stars on opposite sides. Each group counted aloud.
Shot 2 [1:25–1:40]: Animated slide — both groups move toward centre, merge. "Click" SFX.
Shot 3 [1:40–1:55]: Combined group. Numeral "5" appears centre-screen, large.
Shot 4 [1:55–2:15]: Equation "2 + 3 = 5" builds on screen one element at a time.
```

**On-screen text**: "2 + 3 = 5" — each symbol appears as narrated. All spoken simultaneously.
**Key vocabulary introduced**: adding, plus, equals
**Background music**: Continues low, fades during key narration moments.

### Segment 3.2 — Counting On (2:15–3:20)

**Duration**: ~65 seconds
**Concept**: You don't have to start from 1 every time — start from the bigger number and count on.

**Script**:
```
NARRATOR: Here's a helpful trick!

[Kivi appears with the "think" expression, holding up fingers.]

KIVI: "When I add, I don't start at one every time!
I start at the BIGGER number... and count on!"

NARRATOR: Watch. If we have 4 plus 2...
start at 4...

[Large numeral 4 on screen. Two more objects appear one at a time.]

NARRATOR: ...and count on: 5... 6!
4 plus 2 equals 6!

[Equation "4 + 2 = 6" builds on screen.]

NARRATOR: Try it in your head!
What is 5 plus 3?
Start at 5... count on three more...
6... 7... 8!
5 plus 3 equals 8!

[Equation "5 + 3 = 8" builds. Pause 4 seconds for child to process.]
```

**Visual description**:
```
Shot 1 [2:15–2:28]: Kivi in "think" pose with raised paw. Warm, encouraging tone.
Shot 2 [2:28–2:50]: Number line from 1–10. Dot jumps from 4, then two steps forward (4→5→6). Bouncing animation on each jump.
Shot 3 [2:50–3:10]: "4 + 2 = 6" builds on screen. 4-second pause.
Shot 4 [3:10–3:20]: "5 + 3 = 8" builds — child guided to think along. 4-second pause.
```

**On-screen text**: Number line (1–10), equations "4 + 2 = 6" and "5 + 3 = 8". All spoken.
**Key vocabulary**: count on, number line

### Segment 3.3 — Back to Kivi's Apples (3:20–4:48)

**Duration**: ~88 seconds
**Concept**: Apply what was learned to the hook question. Satisfying resolution.

**Script**:
```
NARRATOR: Remember Kivi's apples?

[Return to picnic scene. Apples still mixed together.]

NARRATOR: Kivi had 3 apples in one basket...
and 4 apples in the other.
So what's 3 plus 4?

[Pause 3 seconds.]

NARRATOR: Start at the bigger number — 4!
And count on three more...
5... 6... 7!

[Number line animation — 4 jumps to 5, 6, 7. Each jump is an apple appearing.]

NARRATOR: 3 plus 4 equals 7!
Kivi has 7 apples!

[Kivi cheers. "3 + 4 = 7" appears large on screen. 7 apples arranged beautifully on the picnic blanket.]

NARRATOR: Adding is just putting groups together.
You can do it with apples... stars... coconuts... anything!

[Rapid sequence: 3 quick examples — 1+2=3 (flowers), 2+3=5 (frogs), 4+1=5 (balloons). Each shown for 4 seconds.]

NARRATOR: You already know SO much about adding.
Let's do a quick check!
```

**Visual description**:
```
Shot 1 [3:20–3:35]: Return to picnic. Apples on blanket. Question asked aloud.
Shot 2 [3:35–4:00]: Number line animation. Jumps from 4 → 5 → 6 → 7. Each jump = one apple joins group.
Shot 3 [4:00–4:15]: Kivi celebration (small). "3 + 4 = 7" on screen. 7 apples. Big visual payoff.
Shot 4 [4:15–4:48]: Three rapid examples (each 11 seconds): flowers 1+2=3, frogs 2+3=5, balloons 4+1=5. Fast-paced, fun montage feel.
```

**On-screen text**: "3 + 4 = 7", then rapid equations in the montage. All spoken.

## Production Notes

**Voice casting brief**:
- Narrator: Warm, calm, friendly adult. Think: a favourite teacher. Not a radio announcer. Natural pauses.
- Kivi: Slightly higher, energetic, childlike enthusiasm — but NOT a caricature. Gender-neutral. Warm.

**Animation pace**:
- Allow 3–5 seconds on each new concept before moving on (Grade 1 processing time)
- Number line jumps should be slow enough to count along — max 0.8 seconds per jump
- Equations should build one element at a time, not all at once

**Music spec**:
- Track: Math Mountain world theme (upbeat, adventurous, not frantic)
- Volume: 15–20% under narration, up to 60% in non-dialogue moments
- Full off: when ADHD focus mode is active
- SFX needed: "click/merge" for groups joining, apple bounce (Segment 3.3), celebration sting
