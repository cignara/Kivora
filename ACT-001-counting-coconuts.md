# Counting Coconuts: 1 to 10

---

## Phase 1 — Hook (30 seconds)

> **Kivi says** (KV-002, speech bubble):
> "Kivi found coconuts on the beach! Can you help count them?"

**Scene description**:
A sunny tropical beach fills the screen. Gentle waves lap in the background. Colourful crabs scuttle sideways. Kivi (KV-002) bounces in from the left, carrying a small basket. A pile of coconuts is visible further up the sand — but they're scattered and need counting. The mood is curious and cheerful. Warm yellows, turquoise water, bright sand. No text on screen during the hook.

**Audio narration**:
> "Kivi found something on the beach today — but there are SO many of them! Can you help Kivi count? Touch each coconut as we count together. Ready? Let's go!"

---

## Phase 2 — Warm-Up (2 minutes, 2 interactions, score NOT recorded)

> **Kivi says** (KV-005):
> "Let's warm up first! Touch each crab as Kivi counts!"

### Warm-Up Interaction 1 — Touch to Count (1 to 3)

**Type**: tap-to-select (sequential tap)
**Prompt** (audio only — no on-screen text for this age group):
> "Touch each crab as Kivi counts with you! One… two… three!"
**Visual**: Three large crabs spaced well apart on the sand. Each crab jiggles when tapped. A friendly counting voice says the number aloud as the child taps each one. After all three are tapped, a small star burst appears.
**Touch target size**: Each crab is 120×120px minimum — well above the 64px Toddler requirement.
**Correct response**: Child taps all 3 crabs in any order (one-to-one correspondence, not sequence)
**Kivi on correct** (KV-003): "You did it! Three crabs! Wonderful!"
**Kivi if child taps same crab twice**: Gently plays the number again — no penalty, no negative feedback. The crab shows a small "already counted" sparkle so child can self-correct.

---

### Warm-Up Interaction 2 — Touch to Count (1 to 5)

**Type**: tap-to-select (sequential tap)
**Prompt** (audio):
> "Now five starfish! Touch each one as Kivi counts! One… two… three… four… five!"
**Visual**: Five starfish of varying sizes scattered across the screen. Each lights up bright yellow when tapped and plays its number aloud. A small counter shows how many have been touched (visual only — 1 filled circle per tapped starfish, like a simple tracker).
**Correct response**: Child taps all 5 starfish
**Kivi on correct** (KV-003): "Five starfish! You're a counting star!"
**Kivi if child taps same starfish twice**: Same gentle behaviour as above — sparkle, number replays, no penalty.

## Phase 3 — Core Learning (8 minutes, 10 interactions)

> **Kivi says** (KV-005):
> "Great warm-up! Now let's count Kivi's coconuts — all the way to ten!"

> **Developer note**: No score displayed during this phase. Difficulty stays at Easy throughout (this is an Easy activity). Audio narration reads every number aloud. All tap targets ≥ 64px.

---

### Interaction 1 — Count 1 coconut

**Type**: tap-to-select
**Prompt** (audio): "How many coconuts? Touch the coconut and count with Kivi!"
**Visual**: 1 large coconut sits on the sand, slightly animated (gentle sway). Large enough to be unmissable — approximately 200×200px.
**Correct response**: Child taps the coconut. Kivi's voice counts aloud: "One!"
**Kivi on correct** (KV-003): "One coconut! Great counting!"
**Kivi on 1st wrong** (KV-006): *(Not applicable — there is only one correct thing to tap. If child taps background, Kivi gently says: "Try tapping the coconut!")*
**Hint on 2nd wrong**: Kivi's paw gestures toward the coconut with an arrow.

---

### Interaction 2 — Count 2 coconuts

**Type**: tap-to-select (sequential tap — tap each coconut)
**Prompt** (audio): "Now two! Touch each coconut and count along!"
**Visual**: 2 coconuts, spaced apart. One is slightly bigger than the other (visual variety). Each jiggles on tap and the number plays aloud.
**Correct response**: Child taps both coconuts. Kivi counts: "One… two!"
**Kivi on correct** (KV-003): "Two coconuts! You're amazing!"
**Kivi on 1st wrong**: "Try touching each coconut! One at a time!"
**Hint on 2nd wrong**: Small numbered glowing circles appear above each coconut (1 and 2).

---

### Interaction 3 — Count 3 coconuts

**Type**: tap-to-select (sequential tap)
**Prompt** (audio): "Three coconuts! Touch every single one!"
**Visual**: 3 coconuts arranged in a loose triangle. A friendly crab sits near one (adds life to the scene without distracting). Tapped coconuts display a small ✓.
**Correct response**: All 3 tapped
**Kivi on correct** (KV-003): "Three! Kivi loves counting with you!"
**Kivi on 1st wrong**: "One more coconut is hiding! Find them all!"
**Hint on 2nd wrong**: Unhighlighted coconuts pulse gently.

---

### Interaction 4 — Match the number: "Which group has 4?"

**Type**: tap-to-select (choose the correct group)
**Prompt** (audio): "Which group has FOUR coconuts? Touch the right one!"
**Visual**: Two groups of coconuts shown side by side on two wooden mats.
  - Left mat: 4 coconuts (correct)
  - Right mat: 2 coconuts
  Both groups are clearly separated. Numbers are NOT written — child counts visually.
**Correct response**: Tap the left mat (4 coconuts)
**Kivi on correct** (KV-003): "Four! You counted perfectly!"
**Kivi on 1st wrong** (KV-006): "Hmm! Count each one carefully. How many on that side?"
**Hint on 2nd wrong** (KV-005): Kivi counts the correct group aloud with a pointing paw: "One, two, three, four — this one has four!"

---

### Interaction 5 — Count 5 coconuts (scatter layout)

**Type**: tap-to-select (sequential tap)
**Prompt** (audio): "Five coconuts are hiding on the beach! Can you find and touch them all?"
**Visual**: 5 coconuts scattered across a wider beach scene — some behind rocks, some near the water. Slightly more searching required. Each coconut glows faintly to indicate it can be interacted with. Tapped coconuts pop off the screen with a little bounce.
**Correct response**: All 5 tapped
**Kivi on correct** (KV-004 — mini celebrate): "FIVE! You found them all! Kivi is SO happy!"
**Kivi on 1st wrong**: "Some coconuts are still hiding! Keep looking!"
**Hint on 2nd wrong**: Unhighlighted coconuts emit a gentle sparkle trail.

---

### Interaction 6 — Count 6 coconuts in a row

**Type**: tap-to-select (sequential — left to right encouraged but not required)
**Prompt** (audio): "Six coconuts in a row! Touch each one — one, two, three, four, five, six!"
**Visual**: 6 coconuts in a neat horizontal row on a wooden shelf. This introduces a row/line arrangement — a precursor to number lines.
**Correct response**: All 6 tapped
**Kivi on correct** (KV-003): "Six in a row! Brilliant!"
**Kivi on 1st wrong**: "Try going along the row! Touch each one!"
**Hint on 2nd wrong**: A left-to-right arrow appears, then Kivi points to the first uncounted coconut.

---

### Interaction 7 — Which group has MORE?

**Type**: tap-to-select (comparison — which has more)
**Prompt** (audio): "Which group has MORE coconuts? Touch the bigger group!"
**Visual**:
  - Group A: 4 coconuts
  - Group B: 7 coconuts
  Groups are clearly labelled with visual separators only. No numerals shown.
**Correct response**: Group B (7)
**Kivi on correct** (KV-003): "More coconuts! Great thinking!"
**Kivi on 1st wrong** (KV-006): "Count each group carefully! Which has more?"
**Hint on 2nd wrong** (KV-005): Kivi counts both groups aloud — "This group… one, two, three, four. This group… one, two, three, four, five, six, SEVEN. Which is more?"

---

### Interaction 8 — Count 8 coconuts (two rows)

**Type**: tap-to-select
**Prompt** (audio): "Eight coconuts! They're in two rows now. Touch every one!"
**Visual**: 8 coconuts arranged in 2 rows of 4 (a 2×4 grid layout). This subtly introduces the idea that groups can be arranged in rows — foundational for later addition.
**Correct response**: All 8 tapped
**Kivi on correct** (KV-003): "Eight! Two rows of four — you found them all!"
**Kivi on 1st wrong**: "Check both rows! Make sure you touch every coconut!"
**Hint on 2nd wrong**: Row 1 vs Row 2 visually separated with a gentle highlighted divider.

---

### Interaction 9 — Count and select the matching numeral card

**Type**: tap-to-select (count objects → tap matching number card)
**Prompt** (audio): "Count the coconuts. Then touch the number that matches!"
**Visual**: 9 coconuts arranged in a 3×3 grid. Below them, three large numeral cards: **7**, **9**, **11**. Cards are big (80×80px each) with clear, friendly numerals in Baloo 2 font.
**Correct response**: Tap the "9" card
**Kivi on correct** (KV-003): "Nine! You matched the number perfectly!"
**Kivi on 1st wrong** (KV-006): "Count the coconuts again, slowly! Touch each one."
**Hint on 2nd wrong** (KV-005): Kivi counts aloud as each coconut glows in sequence: "One… two… three… nine! Which card says nine?"

---

### Interaction 10 — Grand finale: Count ALL 10

**Type**: tap-to-select (sequential tap)
**Prompt** (audio): "The last challenge! TEN coconuts — Kivi's whole basket! Count every single one!"
**Visual**: Full screen tropical scene. 10 coconuts arranged in a celebratory pile — a mix of heights and sizes, with a party atmosphere (streamers, tiny sparkles in the background). This is the biggest and most satisfying interaction of the activity.
**Correct response**: All 10 tapped — each one counts aloud as it's tapped (1…2…3…10!)
**Kivi on 10th tap** (KV-004 — full celebrate): A large celebration moment. Confetti falls. Kivi jumps. Stars burst across the screen. Number "10" appears huge in the centre. Music swells for 2 seconds.
**Kivi on 1st wrong**: "So close! Can you find the last one?"
**Hint on 2nd wrong**: Remaining uncounted coconuts pulse with a bright highlight.

## Accessibility Notes

| Profile | This activity's accommodation |
|---|---|
| Toddler (2–3) | Not the primary target (Pre-K activity), but all tap targets are ≥ 64px and audio-first, so it works for a mature 3-year-old with parent present. |
| ADHD | Short-segment mode: deliver Interactions 1–5 only (5 min). Mark as "half complete." Kivi celebrates at Interaction 5 with a "take a break" message. Interactions 6–10 available on return. |
| Dyslexia | No reading required at all. All prompts are audio. The only numerals on screen are in Interaction 9 (3 large numeral cards) — these are shown at high contrast, large size, and spoken aloud. |
| Fine Motor | All interactions are single taps — no drag required. Tap targets ≥ 64px. If child misses a target, the coconut nearest the tap point is selected (generous hit detection radius). |
| Visual | High contrast mode: coconuts outlined in dark `#1E293B` against light sand. Beach background desaturated if high-contrast toggle is on. |
